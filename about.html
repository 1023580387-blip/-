Our Story

Born from the streets. Built for the bold.

The Beginning

VOLT was founded in 2018 in the heart of the city's underground scene. What started as a small screen-printing operation in a basement studio quickly grew into a movement that resonated with a generation tired of fast fashion and hungry for something real.

Our founders weren't fashion school graduates — they were skaters, artists, and musicians who understood that clothing is more than fabric. Clothing is identity. It's a statement without words. Every piece we create carries that DNA.

"We don't follow trends. We set them. Every stitch, every graphic, every silhouette is a middle finger to the ordinary."

From those humble beginnings, VOLT has grown into a global community of creators, rebels, and dreamers. We've collaborated with underground artists, hosted pop-up events in abandoned warehouses, and built a network of supporters who believe in the same ethos: authenticity over everything.

Today, VOLT operates from a design studio in the heart of the city, with distribution reaching over 50 cities worldwide. But we haven't forgotten our roots. Every collection is still designed with the same raw energy and uncompromising attitude that started it all.
